## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an organic, architectural minimalism crafted for modern construction, renovation, and bespoke interior spaces. The aesthetic fuses the tranquility of biophilic design with structural rigor—substituting the raw, unfinished grit of industrial contracting with refined, serene craftsmanship.

The brand targets discerning homeowners, architects, and design-led builders who seek mindful, sustainable transformation. The emotional response is one of grounded assurance, warmth, and measured elegance. Every interaction feels tactile and considered, evoking natural limestone, brushed plaster, and the soft canopy of sage and pistachio foliage. Visual clutter, stark black-and-white contrasts, and industrial high-visibility accents are eliminated in favor of a soothing, botanical warmth.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in soft, sunlit botanical greens and warm, breathable architectural undertones. 

- **Primary Accent (`#6C8A55`)**: A deep, balanced pistachio green providing assertive yet organic grounding for primary interactive elements, active states, and prominent CTA buttons.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8FA382`)**: A soft sage-pistachio mid-tone used for secondary highlights, subtle icon fills, toggle active states, and contextual indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#A9BC9F`)**: A delicate, muted pistachio wash applied to badges, chip backdrops, and active tab indicators.
- **Surface Foundations**:
  - Base Canvas: Warm Linen Chalk (`#F9FAF8`) provides a warm, natural floor.
  - Surface Neutral: Soft Plaster (`#F3F5F0`) creates gentle differentiation for cards, panels, and input surfaces without hard divides.
  - Surface Subtle: Pale Moss Wash (`#EBEFE7`) serves as a muted container background for nested data or highlighted zones.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Muted Pistachio Mist (`#D6DEC3` at variable alpha) delivers soft, tactile enclosure without harsh line-work.
- **Typography & Deep Neutrals**:
  - Primary Content: Deep Botanical Charcoal (`#242B21`) replaces pure black to retain depth while aligning with the olive-pistachio spectrum.
  - Secondary/Supporting Content: Muted Slate Green (`#546050`) ensures accessible readability with soft visual hierarchy.

## Typography

The typographical structure pairs the sculptural, architectural cadence of **Epilogue** for display and headlines with the pristine geometric legibility of **Manrope** for body and interface labels.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (Epilogue)**: Chosen for its deliberate geometry and subtle artisanal warmth. Letter spacing is gently tightened on larger headlines to mimic architectural titling on blueprints and signage.
- **Interface & Prose (Manrope)**: Delivers clear, unpretentious reading across specifications, technical estimates, and body text. The open apertures and steady rhythm maintain clarity against light plaster and pistachio-tinted surfaces.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges**: Set in upper/sentence-case Manrope with delicate letter tracking to ensure clean scannability in technical construction tables and status meters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured 12-column grid system reminiscent of modular architectural framing, balanced by spacious margins that allow photography and technical copy room to breathe.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column fluid grid, 48px (`margin: 3rem`) canvas inset, and 24px (`gutter: 1.5rem`) between structural columns. Generous vertical section rhythms (`space-xl` and above) establish quiet confidence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 32px canvas margins and 20px gutters. Content cards transition to paired 4-column spans.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with 20px (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`) edge offsets and 16px (`gutter-mobile: 1rem`) gutters. Nested components stack into clean single-column flows to maintain minimum touch targets of 44px.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card and component gaps rely strictly on `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled metadata and `space-md` (16px) for form controls and standard card padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on tonal layering and ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient diffusion rather than dark drop shadows or stark black outlines.

- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Base level: Canvas Plaster (`#F9FAF8`).
  - Raised surfaces (cards, sidebars): Elevated Plaster (`#FFFFFF` or `#F3F5F0`).
  - Inset elements (inputs, well modules): Inset Wash (`#EBEFE7`).
- **Ambient Shadow Craft**:
  - Low elevation (Cards, interactive panels): `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(36, 43, 33, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(36, 43, 33, 0.02)`.
  - Mid elevation (Floating headers, tooltips, popovers): `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(36, 43, 33, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 43, 33, 0.03)`.
  - High elevation (Modal dialogs): `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(36, 43, 33, 0.08), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(36, 43, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Soft Muted Borders**: Surfaces use a 1px border colored in soft pistachio slate (`rgba(108, 138, 85, 0.16)`) to subtly delineate structural edges without harsh visual interruptions.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry. 

- UI elements, input fields, and standard buttons feature a disciplined `0.25rem` (4px) border radius.
- Cards, modal containers, and architectural blueprint panels scale smoothly to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Standalone floating modules and image viewports use `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Pill shapes are reserved solely for inline status chips and badges to distinguish operational tags from clickable rectangular elements. This restrained curvature retains the crisp, intentional precision of modern construction detailing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#6C8A55` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 0.25rem border radius. Hover introduces a subtle darkening to `#5F7B4A` with no harsh contrast changes. Focus rings use a 2px offset in `#A9BC9F`.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered in muted pistachio (`1px solid #8FA382`), `#242B21` typography, canvas-matched background. Hover gently shifts the fill to `#F3F5F0`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background, `#546050` text, transitioning to `#EBEFE7` wash on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status Indicators (In Progress, Approved, Renovation Phase)**: Pill-shaped badges using a pale pistachio background (`#EBEFE7`) with deep pistachio-slate text (`#3B4E31`) and a 1px tint border (`#D6DEC3`). Replaces all traditional amber or terracotta indicators with calming sage or muted forest tones.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Fill set to `#FFFFFF` against canvas, enclosed in a 1px `#D6DEC3` perimeter. On focus, the border transitions smoothly to `#6C8A55` with an ambient glow of `rgba(108, 138, 85, 0.15)`. Placeholders rest in `#8FA382` at 60% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Unchecked boxes feature a soft `#D6DEC3` border over `#FFFFFF`. When selected, the control fills with `#6C8A55` bearing a pure white structural check or dot.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed using `#FFFFFF` or `#F3F5F0` bases with a 1px muted pistachio border (`rgba(108, 138, 85, 0.14)`).
- Padding adheres strictly to `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal dividers within cards use a delicate `1px solid #EBEFE7`.

### Architectural Specifics: Project Specification & Estimate Panels
- Tabular figures use Manrope with tabular alignment.
- Progress rails display total milestones with track fills in `#EBEFE7` and active completions advancing in `#6C8A55`.
- Cost and dimension callouts are emphasized through weight hierarchy in deep charcoal (`#242B21`), keeping numbers grounded and legible.
